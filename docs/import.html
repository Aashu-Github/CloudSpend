DETECTION ORDER

Known shapes first.

CloudSpend checks bundle manifests, native EC2/EBS/CloudWatch/Cost Explorer responses, canonical exports, and CUR-like tables before optional AI mapping.

UPLOAD SAFETY

Temporary by default.

Uploads use randomized temporary names, ZIP entries are inspected before extraction, macros are rejected, and raw files are removed after parsing.
